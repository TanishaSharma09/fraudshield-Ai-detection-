# FraudShield AI — Dataset Acquisition & Licensing Guide

## 1. Identified Public Datasets for Indian Digital Payments & UPI Behaviour

Since digital financial institutions cannot release raw, private customer banking records under Indian banking privacy regulations (RBI Guidelines & DPDP Act 2023), fraud detection systems benchmark on **synthetic, privacy-preserving behavioral datasets** that accurately mirror mobile wallet and UPI patterns.

We have identified two major open-source, properly licensed datasets for this purpose:

---

### Dataset Option A: PaySim Synthetic Mobile Money Dataset
- **Description**: PaySim is an internationally recognized mobile money transaction simulator developed by researchers to simulate financial fraud in mobile payments. Mobile money transactions (P2P transfers, merchant payments, debits) mirror Indian UPI workflows (P2P VPA transfers, P2M QR payments, collect requests).
- **Primary Authors**: Edgar Alonso Lopez-Rojas, Ahmad Elmir, and Stefan Axelsson (Blekinge Institute of Technology).
- **Public Repository**: Hosted on Kaggle under `ealaxi/paysim1` ("PaySim Synthetic Financial Datasets For Fraud Detection").
- **Licence**: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — freely usable for academic, commercial, and hackathon benchmarking with attribution.
- **Key Columns**:
  - `step`: Maps to unit of time (1 step = 1 hour of simulation).
  - `type`: Payment mechanism (`TRANSFER`, `PAYMENT`, `CASH_OUT`, `DEBIT`).
  - `amount`: Transfer amount (local currency / INR equivalent).
  - `nameOrig`: Unique customer identifier / sender VPA.
  - `oldbalanceOrg` & `newbalanceOrig`: Account balance delta.
  - `nameDest`: Recipient account / merchant VPA.
  - `oldbalanceDest` & `newbalanceDest`: Recipient balance delta.
  - `isFraud`: Ground-truth binary fraud label (`1` for fraudulent transactions, `0` for legitimate).
  - `isFlaggedFraud`: Heuristic threshold flag (transfers > 200,000).

---

### Dataset Option B: Kaggle UPI / Indian Digital Payment Datasets
- **Description**: Synthetic datasets generated by fintech data science practitioners on Kaggle modeling Indian UPI payments. These datasets include native UPI features such as VPA handle structures (`@okaxis`, `@paytm`, `@okhdfcbank`), device hardware IDs, IP addresses, city geolocations, and time-of-day distributions.
- **Examples**:
  - *"UPI Payments & Fraud Transaction Data"*
  - *"Synthetic Indian Financial Transactions Dataset"*
- **Licence**: Typically **CC0: Public Domain** or **MIT / CC BY 4.0** (check the specific Kaggle card before downloading).
- **Typical Fields**:
  - `transaction_id`, `sender_upi`, `receiver_upi`, `amount`, `channel`, `device_id`, `city`, `timestamp`, `is_fraud`.

## 2. How to Supply a Dataset to FraudShield AI

To supply your dataset for training and evaluation:

1. **Obtain the dataset**:
   - Download the CSV file from Kaggle or your institutional source.
2. **Place the file**:
   - Save the CSV file into the raw dataset directory:
     ```text
     d:\new project\dataset\raw\
     ```
   - *Example destination*: `d:\new project\dataset\raw\upi_transactions.csv` or `d:\new project\dataset\raw\paysim.csv`
3. **Inspect and verify with the Loader**:
   - Run the dataset loader script from your terminal:
     ```powershell
     python -m dataset.loader --path dataset/raw/upi_transactions.csv
     ```
   - Or to inspect the first 10,000 rows of a large dataset:
     ```powershell
     python -m dataset.loader --path dataset/raw/paysim.csv --preview 10000
     ```

## 3. How FraudShield AI Leverages the Data

- **When Ground-Truth Labels Exist (`isFraud` / `is_fraud`)**:
  - The Isolation Forest anomaly scores can be formally calibrated against known fraud incidents to calculate Precision, Recall, and ROC-AUC metrics without data leakage.
- **When Ground-Truth Labels Are Absent (Pure Outlier Detection)**:
  - Unsupervised Isolation Forest isolates extreme multivariate outliers (unusual amount-to-mean ratios, rapid sequential velocity, high-entropy unknown recipients).
