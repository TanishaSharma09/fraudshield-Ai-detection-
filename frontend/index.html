Statistical Baselines • Zero Future Leakage
01 User Spending Baseline

Calculates rolling mean and standard deviation of payment amounts per sender VPA. Amounts exceeding 3.5 σ trigger an anomaly delta score.

02 Rapid Velocity Bursts

Monitors transaction counts across 60-second and 5-minute sliding windows to detect bot-driven account draining or automated testing scripts.

03 Novel Recipient Pairings

Compares destination VPAs against historical recipient sets. High-value transfers to never-before-seen receivers receive elevated risk weights.

04 Device & IP Fingerprints

Tracks device identifier consistency and sudden geographic hops between successive transactions faster than physical travel speeds.

05 Diurnal / Nocturnal Timing

Evaluates hour-of-day versus sender regular habits. Off-peak midnight transactions (01:00 - 05:00 IST) undergo higher isolation scrutiny.

06 Synthetic Mule Ring Cycles

Detects fan-in money collection from multiple accounts quickly followed by fan-out distribution to cash-out destinations in synthetic graphs.
